Privacy Policy

Last updated September 11, 2026

Struck ("we," "us," "the app") is a community map of Chicago matchbooks and the places they came from. This page explains what information we collect, why, and how it's handled.

What we collect

DataWhy
Email addressAccount sign-in (password or emailed link) and password reset.
PasswordStored as an irreversible hash by our auth provider — we never see or store it in plain text.
Photos you uploadMatchbook cover images, read automatically to identify the place and displayed publicly once approved.
Wishlist / been-there marks, commentsPersonalize your experience and are tied to your account.
Basic usage dataStandard web server logs (IP address, browser type) for security and debugging, via our hosting provider.

We do not collect payment information, government ID, or precise device location — location data on the map comes from the address you or the matchbook provide, not your device's GPS.

Who else sees it

We use a small number of service providers to run the app. Each only receives what it needs to do its job:

  • Supabase — hosts our database, file storage, and handles authentication. Your account, photos, and saved lists live here.
  • Anthropic (Claude) — reads the text on a matchbook photo you upload so we can identify the place. The image is sent for that one-time read and is not used to train Anthropic's models under our agreement with them.
  • Google Places (or OpenStreetMap, as a fallback) — looks up an address or business name you search or upload against, so we can plot it on the map accurately.
  • Vercel — hosts the website itself.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone else for advertising.

What's public

Once a submission is approved, the place name, address, category, and any photos you contributed are visible to everyone using the app — that's the point of a shared map. Your email address is never shown publicly. Comments you post on a spot are visible to other signed-in users.

Your choices

  • You can edit or delete your own pending submissions before they're approved.
  • You can request that your account and associated data be deleted by contacting us (below).
  • You can reset your password at any time from the sign-in screen.

Cookies & local storage

We use your browser's local storage to keep you signed in between visits. We don't use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

Children

Struck is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
